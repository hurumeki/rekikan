# Category–Icon Mapping

> Source: `rekikan-data-design.md` — Section 4

---

| category value | Display Label | Icon | Description |
|----------------|---------------|------|-------------|
| era | Era (時代) | 📅 | An era or period division itself |
| law | Politics & Law (政治・法) | ⚖️ | Laws, institutions, political reforms |
| war | War & Diplomacy (戦・外交) | ⚔️ | Wars, treaties, diplomatic incidents |
| culture | Culture & Religion (文化・宗教) | 🎨 | Literature, art, religion, philosophy |
| economy | Economy & Technology (経済・技術) | 💰 | Economic policy, technological innovation, industry |
| person | Person (人物) | 👤 | A specific historical figure as the subject |
| event | Event (出来事) | 📌 | General events that do not fit the categories above |
