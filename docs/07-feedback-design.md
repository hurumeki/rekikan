## 7.1 Post-Answer Display

Common to both modes, the following are displayed after the answer check:

- Correct/incorrect mark for each card (✓ / ✗)
- The correct date
- A brief explanation ("what this era was like")
- Score (X / X correct)
- A "Perfect!" celebration when all answers are correct

## 7.2 Careful Mode-Specific Feedback

- **On a correct answer:** The card moves to the confirmed area, and the date and explanation are displayed
- **On an incorrect answer:** The correct card is highlighted and its explanation is displayed. The incorrectly chosen card remains in the pool of choices
