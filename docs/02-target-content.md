## 2.1 Target Regions

Covers major regions in Japanese history and world history.

| Region | Planned for Initial Release |
|--------|---------------------------|
| Japanese History (日本史) | Era divisions (split + full sequence), Edo-period shoguns, events from the Bakumatsu (幕末) to Meiji (明治), etc. |
| European History (ヨーロッパ史) | Era divisions (split + full sequence) |
| Chinese History (中国史) | Dynasty divisions (split + full sequence) |

## 2.2 Expansion Candidates

- Japanese History: major battles of the Sengoku period (戦国時代), events from Meiji to Shōwa (昭和), cultural history
- European History: addition of medium and fine divisions
- Chinese History: addition of medium and fine divisions
- Other regions: American history, Middle Eastern history, Southeast Asian history
- Cross-region questions (described later)
