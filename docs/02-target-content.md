## 2.1 Target Regions

Covers major regions in Japanese history and world history.

| Region                                            | Planned for Initial Release                                                                                                                                                             |
| ------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Japanese History (日本史)                         | Era divisions (split + full sequence), period events from prehistory to the Heisei era, Ainu and Ryukyu history (アイヌと琉球), medieval society, Edo learning, modern social movements |
| European History (ヨーロッパ史)                   | Era divisions (split + full sequence), Russia and Eastern Europe, early modern international relations, interwar period, Cold War                                                       |
| Chinese History (中国史)                          | Dynasty divisions (split + full sequence), events of each period (ancient, Sui–Tang, Song–Yuan, Ming–Qing, modern)                                                                      |
| West Asia / North Africa (西アジア・北アフリカ史) | Ancient Orient, Islamic world, Ottoman Empire, Safavid and modern Iran, Middle East wars, modern era                                                                                    |
| South Asia (南アジア史)                           | Ancient India, medieval sultanates, Mughal Empire, modern era                                                                                                                           |
| Central Asia (中央アジア史)                       | Nomadic peoples, Turkic states, Islamization, Mongol/Timurid, modern era                                                                                                                |
| Southeast Asia (東南アジア史)                     | Ancient states, port-city kingdoms, Islamization, colonial era, contemporary                                                                                                            |
| Oceania (オセアニア史)                            | Indigenous peoples, European exploration, colonial, federation, contemporary                                                                                                            |
| North America (北アメリカ史)                      | Pre-Columbian, colonization, independence, Civil War & expansion, contemporary, Mesoamerica and Mexico (メソアメリカとメキシコ)                                                         |
| South America (南アメリカ史)                      | Andean civilizations, colonial, independence, republican era, contemporary                                                                                                              |
| Korean Peninsula (朝鮮半島史)                     | Ancient kingdoms, Unified Silla and Balhae, Goryeo, Joseon, opening and colonial rule, division and contemporary                                                                        |
| Africa (アフリカ史)                               | Ancient kingdoms (Kush, Aksum), Saharan and Indian Ocean trade kingdoms, the slave-trade era, partition and resistance, independence and contemporary                                   |

## 2.2 Theme Histories (テーマ史・同時代史)

In addition to per-region histories, the `world` region (`テーマ史・同時代史`) hosts cross-cutting field-based theme histories. Each theme is split into multiple quizzes along connected lineages.

| Theme    | Quiz lineages                                                       |
| -------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 文学     | 西洋古典文学 / 日本古典文学 / 中国古典文学 / 近代小説の系譜         |
| 芸術     | 西洋絵画の様式 / 東アジア絵画の系譜                                 |
| 建築     | 西洋建築様式 / アジアの宗教建築                                     |
| 音楽     | 西洋クラシック音楽の系譜 / 20世紀ポピュラー音楽                     |
| 哲学     | 古代ギリシア哲学 / 近世ヨーロッパ哲学 / 19〜20世紀の思想 / 東洋思想 |
| 法律     | 古代の法典 / 近世・近代法の確立 / 現代の法と国際秩序                |
| 歴史総合 | 大衆化と参政権の拡大 / グローバル化と地球規模の課題                 |

The 歴史総合 lineages follow the high-school 歴史総合 themes (近代化・大衆化・グローバル化) and mix Japanese and world cards in one quiz.

## 2.3 Expansion Candidates

- Japanese History: major battles of the Sengoku period (戦国時代), cultural history
- European History: addition of medium and fine divisions
- Chinese History: neighboring dynasties (Western Xia, Tibet), cultural history
- Additional theme histories: 経済・科学技術・宗教・革命などの拡充, plus new fields (e.g., 数学史, 医学史, スポーツ史)
- Cross-region questions (described later)
